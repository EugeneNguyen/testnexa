# 19 — Blue Ocean Score

**Note on numbering:** requested as "17 — Blue Ocean Score," renumbered to 19 (06–18 already in use). See [00-overview.md](00-overview.md) for the full index.

## Method and caveat

Each of the 20 concepts (15) scored 1–10 on six dimensions. **These scores are a qualitative synthesis (ASSUMPTION-level, not FACT) built by the same evidence already gathered in 01–18** — not an independent measurement, not customer-surveyed, not benchmarked. Treat the *ranking* as directionally useful and the *individual numbers* as illustrative. Validating the top of this ranking against real buyers is the actual next step, not a substitute for it.

**Dimensions:**
- **Customer Value (CV):** how directly this addresses a validated pain/job (02, 03)
- **Differentiation (Diff):** how absent this is from every competitor studied (05, 09, 13)
- **New Demand (ND):** noncustomer-conversion strength, per 18
- **Market Potential (MP):** rough size/reachability of the addressable segment (ASSUMPTION, not sized with primary data)
- **Feasibility (Feas):** buildable by a small/early team given the 07 ERD's complexity
- **Competitive Resistance (CR):** how hard for an incumbent to copy once proven (moat strength)

## Insight

**INSIGHT:** The top 5 (14, 19, 1, 2, 5) cluster tightly (6.7–7.7) and separate cleanly from the rest (next-highest is 6.5, then a long plateau at 6.2). Notably, **the top 5 are not the same five items 18's "clearest new-demand" list highlighted** (which included 3, 4, 10, 12, 13 instead of 2 and 5) — the two analyses ask different questions (18: which noncustomer tier does this pull in; 19: overall weighted strength across value/differentiation/demand/feasibility/moat) and shouldn't be expected to agree exactly. Where they diverge is itself informative: #2 (BYO-LLM) and #5 (continuous traceability) score high here on customer value and differentiation even though 18 didn't flag them as the *purest* new-demand plays — they're strong mainly because they're high-value and hard to copy, not because they open a brand-new buyer category. This distinction matters and is examined directly in 21 (Competition Test).
